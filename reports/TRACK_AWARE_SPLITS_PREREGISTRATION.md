## 1. The finding this amendment exists for

Recorded by the sota-w1 verifier and carried into
`scratchpad/loop/STATE.md`, verbatim:

> Also measured: fray CANNOT wire D6 for the unseen-year track as `splits`
> stands (one splits binding, two tracks with different partitions -> FAIL
> BLOCKS_CONTRADICT_SPLITS). Adoption is more than a repo.toml line.

`resilient-fray` has **two holdout policies over one panel**, and says so in
its own source (`src/validation/yield_holdout.py`, `county_year_splits`):

> IT IS NOT A REPLACEMENT FOR `county_label_splits` AND IT DOES NOT MAKE THAT
> SPLIT'S FIGURES WRONG. The two answer different questions:
> `county_label_splits` measures generalisation to an unseen COUNTY in a year
> the model has seen; this measures generalisation to an unseen future YEAR.

Two policies, two partitions, two different grouping vocabularies (0.5° spatial
block ids; crop years). The `splits` binding in `.mlkit/repo.toml` is **one
key with one return value**, so at most one of them can be declared. D6 ties a
resampling declaration's blocks to `splits[arm]`. Therefore:

* a declaration taken on the **crop-year** track, in a repo whose `splits`
  binding publishes the **county-block** partition, compares crop years
  against block ids and lands on `BLOCKS_CONTRADICT_SPLITS`;
* and the reverse is equally true, so **there is no wiring of `splits` under
  which both of fray's tracks can be judged.**

The consequence is worse than one repo failing one check. The dependence-unit
contract exists *because of* fray's row bootstrap. It is structurally
unusable for exactly the repo whose defect motivated it.

## 2. What is being built

### 2.1 `splits` gains a second admissible shape

1. **Flat — unchanged, and byte-compatible.**
   `{"train": [...], "val": [...], "test": [...]}`.
2. **Tracked — new.** A mapping whose **only** key is the reserved word
   `tracks`, mapping each track name to a flat splits mapping:
   `{"tracks": {"<name>": {"train": [...], "val": [...], "test": [...]}, ...}}`.

Discrimination is by the reserved key **and by that key being the mapping's
only key** — never by "the values look nested". A binding returning
`{"train": {"a": 1}}` is a flat splits whose train group ids are the keys of a
dict; guessing at nesting from value types would silently reinterpret it.

`normalise_tracked_splits` is the one reader, and it delegates each track to
the existing `normalise_splits`, so the character-splitting defect that parser
was written to refuse (a `str` iterated into one group per letter) is refused
inside every track by the same code. Rule 7 inside one package: two readings of
"what the splits binding said" is the failure mode where the second copy is the
one nobody remembers to fix.

### 2.2 `R3` judges every track by every clause it applies today

Under a tracked `splits`, R3 applies **each** of its existing clauses — the
three arms present, pairwise disjointness, no empty arm, and the
`MIN_HOLDOUT_GROUPS` holdout floor — to **each track**, and the verdict is the
worst. No clause is relaxed for any track and no threshold moves.

### 2.3 `ResamplingDeclaration` gains a seventh DECLARED field: `track`

`track` is **declared, not derived**, and that is not an exception to the rule
that made the counts and the digests `init=False`. Those are facts about the
assignment, and the assignment is their only admissible source. `track` is a
**pointer into another binding** — the assignment does not contain, and cannot
contain, the name a different callable gave a partition. Deriving it would mean
mlkit guessing which of a repo's partitions this declaration meant, which is
mlkit choosing the operand of its own verdict.

Default `""` = "this repo has one, unnamed, track". `to_dict()` emits `track`
**only when it is non-empty**, so every current adopter's evidence bytes are
unchanged.

### 2.4 `resampling_declaration` may return a SEQUENCE of declarations

A two-track repo has two intervals. A binding that could only ever return one
declaration would leave the second track's interval unjudged and silent, which
is the shape of defect this whole branch exists to end. So the binding may
return either one mapping (today, unchanged) or a sequence of them. Overall
status is the worst of the per-declaration verdicts.

### 2.5 D6 resolves the declaration against its own track

| declaration `track` | `splits` shape | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| `""` | flat | today's path, byte-for-byte |
| `""` | tracked | **FAIL `TRACK_UNDECLARED`** |
| `"X"` | flat | **FAIL `TRACK_NOT_IN_SPLITS`** |
| `"X"` | tracked, `X` absent | **FAIL `TRACK_NOT_IN_SPLITS`** |
| `"X"` | tracked, `X` present | blocks tied to `splits.tracks.X[arm]` |

`TRACK_UNDECLARED` is the load-bearing one. Given N tracks and a declaration
that names none, mlkit could pick the track whose blocks happen to match and
report PASS. That is a check selecting its own operand — E-M21's shape — and it
is refused instead.

## 3. THE AMENDMENT IS STRICTLY STRICTER. Stated as a claim to be attacked.

**Claim.** Every input that produces FAIL at `24f23b8` produces FAIL at this
branch's head, and no threshold, range, holdout floor or existing assertion is
edited. Concretely:

1. `MIN_HOLDOUT_GROUPS` is not read, moved or bypassed; under tracks it is
   applied **once per track** instead of once.
2. Every refusal in `ResamplingDeclaration`'s ladder
   (`BLOCKS_STRADDLE_ARMS`, `SINGLE_UNIT`, `DEPENDENCE_UNIT_TOO_FINE`,
   `UNIT_LABEL_CONTRADICTS_CONTENT`) is untouched and fires on the same inputs.
3. Four refusals are **added** (`TRACKS_MIXED_WITH_FLAT`, `TRACKS_MALFORMED`,
   `TRACKS_ARE_THE_SAME_PARTITION`, `TRACK_UNDECLARED`/`TRACK_NOT_IN_SPLITS`,
   `DUPLICATE_TRACK_DECLARATION`). None replaces an existing one.
4. The tracked shape FAILs R3 at `24f23b8` (`splits missing: train, val,
   test`). At head it can PASS — **only** by satisfying every clause on every
   track. A shape that could not be expressed is now expressible; nothing that
   could be expressed got easier.

**Where the claim is weakest, said before it is tested.** Point 4 turns a FAIL
into a possible PASS for one shape. That is the whole item, and it is the one
place a reviewer should look hardest. The control for it is §5's B-3: the
tracked envelope with a track that violates any R3 clause must still FAIL, and
the two-track fixture must FAIL if either track alone would.

## 4. What this does NOT establish

* **mlkit does not compare group ids across tracks.** Two tracks are two
  grouping vocabularies (fray: block ids and crop years — both integers, and
  an equal integer means nothing). A "group X is train here and test there"
  refusal would be a category error dressed as rigour. The count of shared
  stringified ids is **recorded in evidence and interpreted nowhere.**
  `TRACKS_ARE_THE_SAME_PARTITION` is the one exception and it is deliberately
  narrow: all three arms equal as sets, which is not a coincidence between two
  real vocabularies, and which means the repo declared one partition twice.
* **A track in `splits` with no declaration is recorded, not refused.** mlkit
  cannot know whether a track produced an interval. The uncovered track names
  go in D6's evidence under `tracks_without_declaration`, and this
  pre-registration states plainly that it is a **recorded gap**, not a check.
* **`UNIT_CROSSCUTS_ARMS` still silences `DEPENDENCE_UNIT_TOO_FINE`.** That is
  the caveat the sota-w1 verifier raised against PR #32 and it is item I1-M1's
  target, not this one. This branch does not touch that ladder. The
  consequence for CONTROL A is stated in §6 rather than discovered later.

## 6. What CONTROL A can and cannot show at this head — declared in advance

CONTROL A as specified says the crop-year track must refuse "row/county"
units. Split in two, because the two halves have different owners:

* **`unit="row"` — this branch's to demonstrate.** A row unit stays inside the
  arm and splits crop-year blocks, so `DEPENDENCE_UNIT_TOO_FINE` fires. This
  must hold at head.
* **`unit="county"` — expected to stay SILENT at this head, and reported as
  not closed.** A county contributes rows to train, val and test years under
  the crop-year policy, so `UNIT_CROSSCUTS_ARMS` is the derived relation, and
  at `24f23b8` that relation silences the refusal unconditionally. That is
  precisely the caveat item **I1-M1** was spawned to close. I1-M1 had **not
  pushed a branch** when this one was cut (`git ls-remote` at spawn, recorded
  in the results file), so this branch is cut from `24f23b8` directly and
  cannot depend on the repair. **The county half of CONTROL A is predicted to
  fail here, is predicted now rather than explained afterwards, and the
  measured outcome is reported either way.**

## 7. Ledgered reads

All four ledgered test reads stay HELD. This branch computes **no metric, no
prediction and no residual on any row of any `test` partition** in any repo.
Split *membership* — which group ids are in `test` — is what R3 and D6 already
read today through the `splits` binding on `main`, and reading it is not a
test read. The fray drive calls `county_label_splits()` and
`county_year_splits()` for membership only; no model is loaded and no label of
a test row reaches a metric.

## 8. What would refute this branch

* any input that FAILs at `24f23b8` and does not FAIL at head;
* any change to a threshold, range, holdout floor, or existing assertion;
* a single-track adopter whose D6/R3 status, reason or evidence differs
  between base and head;
* a two-track repo whose declaration can be judged against a partition it did
  not name;
* the full mlkit suite not green at head.
