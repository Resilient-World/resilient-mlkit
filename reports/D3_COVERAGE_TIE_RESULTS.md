## 3. CONTROL C — check-not-dead

14 mutations, one guard at a time, each file restored afterwards with its
sha256 asserted byte-identical (`reports/d3_coverage_tie/mutations.json`).

**13 caught on the first pass. One survived, and it is worth more than the 13.**
Rewriting `n, empirical = derived.n, derived.empirical` back to the reported
figures left 113 tests green — because past the tie the two agree, exactly as
the code comment predicted. The one place they come apart is a binding
declaring a tolerance BELOW the representation allowance, which D3 permits
because a subject may ask for something stricter than mlkit and never looser:
rows saying 0.90 exactly, a reported `0.90 + 5e-13` (inside the allowance, so
the tie is silent), `tol = 1e-13` — PASS on the derived figure, FAIL on the
reported one. `test_the_coverage_verdict_is_taken_on_the_derived_empirical`
pins it and the mutation now dies. **14/14.**

The `n` half of that same assignment is **not observable and no test pretends
otherwise**: `n` must equal the recount exactly to get past the tie, so the two
are the same integer by construction.

## 5. What is NOT closed

Written in the preregistration before the measurement and unchanged by it:

1. **The rows are still the subject's.** A binding that generates rows to match
   a figure satisfies this contract — the fixtures in this branch do exactly
   that, deliberately, in the test file and never in `src/`. The next tie is a
   `row_set_digest` declared in COMMITTED state beside `[coverage] nominal`,
   which is the shape the level itself now has. OPEN, unassigned, recorded in
   `docs/ESCALATIONS.md` E-M34.
2. **The group form ties to a declared partition, not to rows.** Internally
   consistent fabricated group counts are refused by nothing here.
3. **Adopters move PASS → NA on unchanged code.** No in-scope repo moves today
   (none reaches D3), but the colleague-owned repos were not driven and are not
   this agent's to change. arabica is the repo whose disclosed shortfall E-M21
   records being erased and is the likeliest to hold a live D3 row.
4. **A `numpy.bool_` `covered` flag is refused by name.** mlkit will not take a
   numpy dependency on for eight repos to recognise one; the adopter's fix is
   `bool(x)` at the yield site.
