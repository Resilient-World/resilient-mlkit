## 1. The finding this instrument exists for

Round-8 adjudication, §2.1 (`scratchpad/loop/adjudication.md`), measured in
`resilient-fray`:

> fray's holdout policy, in its own words, is that **whole crop years go to one
> partition**. The exchangeable unit is therefore the crop year, and VAL has
> **five** of them. The run's bootstrap resamples **1,365 rows** as if
> independent.

| resampling unit | point | 95% CI | clears zero |
|---|---:|---|---|
| ROW — what the run reported | +22.811 | [+16.016, +29.646] | yes |
| CROP-YEAR block (5 clusters) | +22.811 | **[−1.289, +41.704]** | **NO** |

`resilient-chokepoint` resamples its dependence unit (corridor block, 28
clusters, predictions held fixed). fray resampled rows. **No gate was edited by
anyone**: fray's preregistration fixed the row bootstrap in advance and the run
honoured it exactly. The defect is that the fleet held two conventions and
**nothing in mlkit required either one, or required the choice to be stated.**

That is the gap this branch closes: not "fray was wrong", but "an interval
could rest on a resampling unit nobody declared, and no instrument compared
that unit to the holdout policy the same artifact declares."

## 2. What is being built

1. **`core.served.RowUnit`** — one row of the panel, carrying four things and
   nothing else: its row key, the arm it is in, the key of the block the
   holdout policy keeps whole, and the key of the unit the resampling procedure
   drew.

2. **`core.served.ResamplingDeclaration`** — the declaration. It names the
   procedure, the number of draws, the holdout policy, the policy's blocking
   unit, the unit resampled, and the arm. Everything else — the row/block/unit
   counts, the three `row_set_digest` ties, the relation between the unit and
   the blocks, and the refusal — is **derived in `__post_init__` from the
   assignment and is `init=False`**. Following `Comparison.row_matched` (M-06):
   a caller cannot spell the digest, cannot spell the counts, and cannot spell
   the verdict. The only input is content.

3. **The contradiction rule.** Stated in full, before it is coded:

   > A resampling procedure draws units and treats them as exchangeable. A
   > holdout policy that keeps a block whole has asserted that the block's rows
   > are **not** exchangeable — that is the whole reason it refuses to split
   > them. So: **if the unit the procedure drew stays inside the deciding arm
   > and splits at least one of the policy's blocks, the procedure has
   > manufactured independent replicates out of exactly the rows the policy
   > refused to separate.** That is a contradiction between two declarations in
   > one artifact, and mlkit refuses it, naming both units and both counts.

   Three further derived relations, none of which is a refusal:

   * `UNIT_IS_THE_BLOCK` — the unit is the policy's blocking unit. fray's
     repair.
   * `UNIT_COARSER_THAN_BLOCK` — whole blocks nest inside units. Conservative.
   * `UNIT_CROSSCUTS_ARMS` — the unit's keys appear in more than one arm, so it
     is an axis the split does not partition at all. chokepoint's corridor.

4. **What this contract does NOT establish, stated here so it is not read as
   more than it is.** `UNIT_CROSSCUTS_ARMS` is *recorded*, not blessed. A
   corridor bootstrap does not account for the temporal axis the time-blocked
   split partitions, and this instrument does not claim it does. What it does
   is force both numbers into the record side by side — the units resampled and
   the blocks in the arm that were not — so that a reader of chokepoint's
   ladder sees `n_units = 28, n_blocks_in_arm = <k>` rather than "corridor
   bootstrap". Refusing the crosscutting case as well would refuse the
   convention the adjudication endorsed, on a rule the adjudication did not
   support, and inventing that standard here is not this branch's to do.

5. **`Comparison` gains an interval.** Three optional fields —
   `skill_interval_low`, `skill_interval_high`, `resampling` — and four
   construction refusals that cannot be stepped around:
   * an interval with no `ResamplingDeclaration` raises. An interval is the
     output of a resampling procedure and there is no way to declare one
     without declaring what it drew.
   * a `ResamplingDeclaration` with no interval raises. A declaration about
     nothing is not a declaration.
   * one endpoint without the other, a non-finite endpoint, or `low > high`
     raises.
   * an interval that does not contain its own point estimate raises. Every
     operand needs a tie, and an interval on a different quantity than the one
     the gate decides is untied while looking tied (tick-13's lesson).

6. **`challenger_decision` gains three lanes**, and the existing eight are
   untouched in order and in verdict:
   * `RESAMPLING_ROWS_UNTIED` (NA) — the declaration's row digest is not the
     comparison's candidate row digest. The interval and the point estimate are
     about different rows.
   * `DEPENDENCE_UNIT_CONTRADICTS_POLICY` (NA) — the declaration refuses.
   * `INTERVAL_COVERS_ZERO` (FAIL) — asked **only after** the point estimate
     has already cleared, so no comparison without an interval changes verdict.
     This is the fray case exactly: point `+22.811` clears; the crop-year-block
     interval `[−1.289, +41.704]` does not.

7. **`ChallengerDecision.to_dict()` emits a top-level `resampling` key.** It is
   the string `"NA"` when no comparison carried a declaration. Silence becomes a
   printed absence in the record rather than a missing key nobody notices.

8. **`D6 RESAMPLING_UNIT`** — a new check in the `decision` phase, appended to
   `PHASE_ORDER["decision"]` after `D5`. It resolves a new
   `resampling_declaration` binding, builds the declaration **inside mlkit**
   from the content the binding returns, and then does the thing the
   declaration alone cannot: it ties the declared blocks to the `splits`
   binding **R3 already reads**. A repo that fabricates its blocking unit
   inside the declaration (`block_key = row_key`, which makes the declaration
   self-consistent and silent) is caught here, because `splits()` is a second,
   separately-judged declaration of the same partition.

   Verdicts: FAIL when the declaration refuses; FAIL when the declared blocks
   disagree with `splits()`; NA when either binding is absent or the blocks
   cannot be tied; PASS only when the declaration is clean **and** tied.

## 3. Hypotheses, stated before measuring

* **H1 (fires).** A fray-shaped assignment — five crop-year blocks in `val`,
  1,365 row units — yields `DEPENDENCE_UNIT_TOO_FINE`, and the message names
  `crop_year`, `row`, `5` and `1365`.
* **H2 (silent on the repair).** The same rows with `unit = crop_year` yield no
  refusal and relation `UNIT_IS_THE_BLOCK`.
* **H3 (silent on chokepoint's convention).** Corridor keys present in `train`,
  `val` and `test` yield no refusal and relation `UNIT_CROSSCUTS_ARMS`, with
  `n_blocks_in_arm` reported beside `n_units`.
* **H4 (the constructing layer holds).** `ResamplingDeclaration(...,
  unit_digest=...)`, `n_units=...` or `refusal=...` is a `TypeError` naming the
  argument. There is no spelling of the derived facts.
* **H5 (the decision path).** With a clean declaration, an interval covering
  zero is `FAIL / INTERVAL_COVERS_ZERO` while its point estimate is strictly
  positive; an interval clearing zero is `PASS`.
* **H6 (nothing that exists moves).** Every comparison that carries no interval
  produces the byte-identical decision it produced at `6921e9a`, across all
  eight existing lanes. The readiness, triage, selection and economics phases
  are untouched; `PHASE_ORDER["decision"]` gains one id and reorders nothing.
* **H7 (D6 on the fleet, predicted before running).** D6 is expected to be
  **NA on every repo on disk**, because no repo declares a
  `resampling_declaration` binding yet — this branch defines the contract and
  adopts it nowhere. **A PASS anywhere in that run would be evidence the check
  is blind, not evidence a repo is clean**, and would be reported as such.

## 4. What would falsify this work

- H1 failing: the instrument cannot be made to fire on the exact assignment the
  adjudication measured.
- H3 failing: the contract refuses chokepoint's corridor bootstrap, which would
  make the fleet's *correct* convention unadoptable — the R12 failure mode
  ("adopting the check would not clear it").
- H6 failing: any existing lane of `challenger_decision` changing status,
  refusal class or reason on a comparison with no interval.
- The `block_key = row_key` fabrication reaching a PASS through D6 with a
  `splits` binding present and disagreeing.

## 5. Explicitly out of scope

- **No adopter repo is edited.** fray and chokepoint adopt this on their own
  branches, behind their own verifiers. fray PR #78 and chokepoint PR #101 are
  open and unmerged; this branch touches no file either of them touches.
- **No R12 clause.** Re-implementation detection for the new surface is a
  separate question and a fleet-wide scanner change; adding one here would ship
  an unmeasured scanner clause alongside an unadopted contract.
- **No threshold on the number of units.** "Five clusters is too few" is a
  judgement this instrument does not make and has no basis to make. It reports
  `n_units` and `n_blocks_in_arm`; the interval's own width is the argument.
  The single arithmetic refusal is `n_units < 2`, which is not a threshold: one
  unit cannot be resampled at all.
- **No tag, no version bump.** `v0.5.0` is untagged; this appends a section to
  its notes.
- **No test that exists on `main` is weakened, edited or deleted.**
